PlanWith Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

한국어 · English · 日本語

PlanWith (the "app") handles as little of your personal information as possible. This page explains what the app handles, why, and how you can delete it.

In short: your tasks stay on your device by default and are not sent to our servers. Only when you use the friends feature is some information stored on a server, and you can delete it from inside the app at any time.

1. Information the app handles

a. Stored on your device only (never sent to our servers)

  • The tasks, notes, categories, and attached photos you create
  • If you have iCloud turned on, the above syncs through your own iCloud account (Apple holds it; we cannot see it).
  • This app does not use location. There is no screen that asks for location permission.

b. Stored on our server when you use the friends feature

  • Anonymous account identifier — a random value generated automatically per device for the friends feature and anonymous statistics. On its own it is not linked to your name, email, or phone number.
  • Social sign-in information — if you sign in with Apple, Kakao, or Google to use the friends feature, the account identifier and email address that service gives us are stored on the authentication server. We never receive your password. (With Sign in with Apple, choosing Hide My Email stores a relay address instead of your real one.)
  • Profile — display name, @username, and optionally a profile photo
  • Your friend list, friend requests sent and received, and users you have blocked
  • Events you have explicitly set to "share with friends" (title, date, completion state). Tasks you have not shared are never uploaded.
  • Push notification device token — a per-device identifier issued by Apple, used to deliver friend request and acceptance notifications. It is no longer used once you turn notifications off or delete the app.
  • App language of the device — stored alongside the push token so that notifications arrive in the language you use the app in. It is used for nothing else.

c. Information used to improve the app

  • Anonymous usage statistics — only event names such as app launch or task creation are recorded. The contents of your tasks are never included.
  • Feedback — the message you write, plus the app version, iOS version, and device model to help us reproduce problems

2. Why we use this information

  • To provide adding, requesting, and accepting friends, and viewing each other's shared schedules
  • To find problems and improve usability (anonymous statistics and feedback)

We do not use your information for advertising and we do not sell it to third parties. The app contains no advertising or tracking SDKs.

3. Who can see your information

  • Shared events are visible only to friends you have accepted. You can also turn on "hide my schedule" per friend so that one person cannot see them.
  • Your profile (display name, @username, profile photo) is visible to your friends and to anyone who has sent or received a friend request using your @username. You can block anyone you would rather not hear from; blocking stops them from sending requests.
  • Server data is stored with Supabase (our backend provider) and processed only to operate the service.
  • We do not hand your information to anyone else except where required by law.

4. Retention and deletion

  • Delete account — tapping Settings → Account & Data → "Delete account" in the app deletes the sign-in account itself and everything attached to it (email and account identifier, profile, profile photo, friend list, requests sent and received, shared events, block list, push notification device token) from the server. This cannot be undone.
  • Reset the app — "Delete all tasks" in Settings removes every task on this device.
  • Delete the app — deleting the app removes the task data stored on your device with it.
  • Deleted tasks in the Trash are permanently removed automatically once the retention period you chose (30 days by default) has passed.

5. Children's privacy

This app is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

6. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the "Last updated" date above.

7. Contact

For privacy questions, use Settings → Send feedback in the app, or email us.
